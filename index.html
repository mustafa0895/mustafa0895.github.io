The Story

Every studentdeserves aEureka moment.

I teach math and science, one student at a time. When a worksheet wasn't enough, I started building lessons students can play with: drag a slider and watch the rain change a savanna, test a prediction, earn a certificate. Now I design and build every piece myself, from the logo to the last quiz question.

  • 01

    Show, don't tell

    If a student can move it, test it or break it, they remember it. Every idea gets something to interact with.

  • 02

    Two languages, one idea

    English for the curriculum, Arabic one tap away. Language should never stand between a student and the science.

  • 03

    Start close to home

    Our seas, our deserts, the oryx and the ghaf tree. Examples from the UAE make big ideas feel near.

  • 04

    Design is teaching

    A clear page is a clear explanation. I design my materials as carefully as I plan the lesson.
